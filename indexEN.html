Privacy Policy for the "Moja Parafia" Mobile Application

Last updated: August 27, 2026

1. General Provisions

Moja Parafia ("the app") is an independent, free directory of Catholic parishes. It is not an official app of any parish, diocese, or the Catholic Church. There is no user account and no login. The Data Administrator is Michał Schmude, operating at mivs.dev, email: biuro.mojaparafia@gmail.com (hereinafter referred to as the "Administrator").

2. What We Collect

  • Per-install identifier. A device-generated identifier (identifierForVendor on iOS, ANDROID_ID on Android) is sent to our server (api-parafia.mivs.dev) so that we can attach your push-notification token and remember your "home parish" and favourite parishes. It is not linked to your name or any account.
  • Push notification token (Firebase Cloud Messaging), used to send notifications about Mass reminders and parish news.
  • Usage analytics (Firebase Analytics): which screens and features are opened, app version, device model, operating-system version, and an approximate region derived from your IP address. This is aggregate product analytics. We do not track you across other apps or websites, and the app shows no advertising.
  • Content you submit. If you propose adding or correcting a parish, we receive the data you type. These submissions are reviewed by us before they can appear in the app.
  • Parish and clergy data: parish name, address, office email, phone number, and the parish priest's name. This is publicly available data obtained from official diocese websites of the Catholic Church and parish notice boards.

3. What Stays on Your Device

  • Location (GPS). When you grant location permission, your position is used only on the device to find the nearest parish and to centre the map. It is processed "live" and is never sent to our servers.
  • Mass reminders you create are stored only on your device.
  • App settings (language, theme, notification preference).

4. What We Do NOT Collect

Name, e-mail address (unless you choose to e-mail our support address), password, payment or financial information, contacts, photos, precise stored location history, or the advertising identifier (IDFA / GAID). The app contains no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, and no advertising.

5. Legal Basis and Purpose of Processing

  • Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (Legitimate Interest): providing the informational service (parish data) and ensuring application stability and security.
  • Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (Consent): for data voluntarily provided by users (change-proposal forms).
  • General Decree on the Protection of Personal Data of Individuals in the Church: regarding the publication of public information about clergy functions.

6. Data Security

  • Encryption of connections using the HTTPS (SSL/TLS) protocol.
  • Data minimization – we only collect what is necessary for the application to function.

7. Data Recipients (Third-Party Services)

To provide its functionality, the app integrates the following services:

  • Google Maps (Android) / Apple Maps (iOS): map display and navigation.
  • Firebase (Cloud Messaging, Analytics): push notifications and aggregate product analytics.

We do not sell personal data. The app does not use advertising SDKs and does not process any payments.

8. Rights of Data Subjects

According to GDPR, every user has the right to:

  • Access, rectify, and delete their data ("right to be forgotten").
  • Object to the processing of location data (via the device's system settings).
  • File a complaint with the supervisory authority (President of the Personal Data Protection Office - PUODO).

Contact for privacy matters: biuro.mojaparafia@gmail.com.

9. "Suggest a Change" Feature

Data provided in the change-proposal form is used solely to verify the reliability of the information and is reviewed by us before it can appear in the app. Providing proposals is completely voluntary.

10. Data Retention Period

  • Location data: not retained (processed only in memory while the map is active).
  • E-mail addresses from forms: until the change is verified (maximum 12 months).
  • Push tokens and parish preferences: kept while the app is installed and for a short period afterwards.
  • Parish data: for the duration of the informational service.

To request deletion of data associated with your device, e-mail biuro.mojaparafia@gmail.com.

11. Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

12. Changes to the Privacy Policy

The Administrator reserves the right to make changes to this document. The current version is always available at mivs.dev and within the application.
